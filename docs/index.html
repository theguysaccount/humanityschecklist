    Every world problem · the base of the triangle

      Why the source

      Band-Aids don't scale

      Most of the world's good work fights problems one at a time. Each of those problems is real, and most of them keep coming back. Solve the six sources on this list and every other problem becomes solvable.

      Symptoms

      Climate change, war, poverty, loneliness. Each one gets its own charities, summits and budgets, and each is treated as if it stood alone.

      Sources

      They grow from a few shared roots: the story we tell, how we coordinate, what we can picture, what our feeds reward, what we admire and who gets to build.

      The flip

      Fix a source and it stops feeding the disease and starts feeding the cure. Each item speeds up the other five, and the pattern repeats at every scale, from one person to a city to a species. The dominoes are fractal.

      Beachheads

      Every item comes with a beachhead: one focused project that wins a foothold on its source. Six footholds open the road to protopia, a world that gets a little better every year.

      The math

      What if $5 million was all it took?

      Hundreds of billions of dollars go to charity every year. What if $5 million, spent correctly, was all it actually took? The world doesn't have a charity problem. It has a coordination, vision and status problem. The resources exist, and no amount of funding for Band-Aids will ever heal the core.
